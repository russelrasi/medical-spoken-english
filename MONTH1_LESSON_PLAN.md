# Month 1 Lesson Plan: Medical Student Spoken English

Learner: Samira. Twelve classes over four weeks. Each class is about 60 minutes of guided work plus 15 minutes of daily homework.

## How each class runs

| Minutes | Activity | In the app |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 | Warm-up: say three sentences from the previous class aloud | Speaking practice (listen, repeat) |
| 5 to 12 | Watch the explainer once, then pause and repeat the key sentences | Class sheet, Watch explainer |
| 12 to 20 | Read the guidebook: rules, examples, mistakes | Guidebook |
| 20 to 40 | Lesson: 10 mixed questions, then review the report | Start lesson |
| 40 to 50 | Sound corner and Medical corner words, said aloud | Guidebook, Sound corner and vocabulary |
| 50 to 60 | Record one voice message of the speaking sentences and send it to the teacher on WhatsApp | Speaking practice |
| Homework | 15 minutes: the 100 mixed exercises, 20 to 30 per day, plus 5 minutes of speaking | Homework button |

Use the end-of-lesson report as the teacher's checklist: slow questions show hesitation, mistakes link to the guidebook rule.

## Week 1

### Class 1: Self-Introduction & the Verb 'To Be'

- **Focus:** Subject + Verb + Object; am / is / are
- **Goals:** Introduce yourself with am, is and are. Use five classroom phrases to ask for help. Say the sounds v, w and th more clearly. Use twelve first-week college and hospital words.
- **Words:** Student, Anatomy, Lecture, Patient, Hospital, Ward, Library, Textbook, Hostel, Teacher, Stethoscope, Classmate
- **Medical corner:** built into the word list (anatomy, patient, ward, stethoscope)
- **Sound corner:** v and w, and the th sound.
- **Dialogue:** Samira meets her anatomy teacher before the first lecture.
- **Speaking:** 5 sentences to record
- **In the app:** explainer 1:32, 80 exercises in the pool, homework of 100 mixed items.

### Class 2: Family & Personal Information

- **Focus:** have / has; possessive adjectives (my, your, his, her, our, their)
- **Goals:** Use have with I, you, we, they and has with he, she, it. Use the correct possessive adjective, especially his for a male and her for a female. Describe her own family in eight connected sentences.
- **Words:** Father, Mother, Siblings, Elder / Younger, Doctor, Engineer, Housewife, Relatives, Married
- **Medical corner:** Head, Chest, Arm, Leg, Heart, Lungs
- **Sound corner:** the h in have and has.
- **Dialogue:** Two students talking in the college canteen during a break.
- **Speaking:** 5 sentences to record
- **In the app:** explainer 1:24, 102 exercises in the pool, homework of 100 mixed items.

### Class 3: Daily Routine & the Present Simple Tense

- **Focus:** Present Simple; the -s ending for he / she / it; do and does
- **Goals:** Describe habits and routines using the Present Simple. Add -s to the verb for he, she and it, and remove it after does. Talk about a typical college day for one and a half minutes.
- **Words:** Wake up, Brush, Attend, Lecture, Study, Return, Revise, Sleep, Usually
- **Medical corner:** Pulse, Temperature, Blood pressure, Dose, Tablet, Rest
- **Sound corner:** the three sounds of the -s ending.
- **Dialogue:** A senior student asks the new student about her daily schedule in the hostel corridor.
- **Speaking:** 4 sentences to record
- **In the app:** explainer 1:30, 87 exercises in the pool, homework of 100 mixed items.

- **Week check (10 minutes):** after this class, do a speaking round of 5 questions covering Classes 1 to 3; note slow answers.

## Week 2

### Class 4: Present Continuous: What Is Happening Now

- **Focus:** am / is / are + verb-ing; Present Simple vs Present Continuous
- **Goals:** Form the Present Continuous with am, is or are plus verb-ing. Tell the difference between a habit and an action happening right now. Answer 'What are you doing?' naturally on a phone call.
- **Words:** Preparing, Reading, Waiting, Listening, Cooking, Walking, At the moment, Right now
- **Medical corner:** Breathing, Coughing, Bleeding, Sleeping, Healing, Recovering
- **Sound corner:** the -ing ending.
- **Dialogue:** A phone call between two classmates in the evening.
- **Speaking:** 5 sentences to record
- **In the app:** explainer 1:27, 98 exercises in the pool, homework of 100 mixed items.

### Class 5: Likes, Dislikes & Hobbies

- **Focus:** like / love / enjoy / hate + verb-ing or noun; prefer
- **Goals:** Use like, love, enjoy and hate followed by verb-ing or a noun. Express a preference with prefer ... to ... Hold a two-minute conversation about free time and food.
- **Words:** Spare time, Enjoy, Dislike, Prefer, Favourite, Cuisine, Hobby, Boring, Interesting
- **Medical corner:** Appetite, Exercise, Diet, Sleep, Stress, Healthy
- **Sound corner:** word stress.
- **Dialogue:** Two students getting to know each other over lunch.
- **Speaking:** 5 sentences to record
- **In the app:** explainer 1:29, 93 exercises in the pool, homework of 100 mixed items.

### Class 6: WH-Questions & Prepositions of Time

- **Focus:** What, Where, When, Why, Who, Which, How; at / in / on for time
- **Goals:** Form questions with the seven WH-words in the correct word order. Choose at, in or on correctly for clock times, months and days. Ask a senior student five questions to get real information.
- **Words:** Schedule, Timetable, Midterm, Deadline, Weekend, Notice board, How often, How long
- **Medical corner:** Clinic, Laboratory, Pharmacy, Emergency room, Reception, Appointment
- **Sound corner:** question intonation.
- **Dialogue:** The new student asks a third-year senior about the department schedule.
- **Speaking:** 6 sentences to record
- **In the app:** explainer 1:30, 98 exercises in the pool, homework of 100 mixed items.

- **Week check (10 minutes):** after this class, do a speaking round of 5 questions covering Classes 4 to 6; note slow answers.

## Week 3

### Class 7: Yesterday & Past Experiences: Past Simple

- **Focus:** was / were; regular verbs with -ed
- **Goals:** Use was with I, he, she, it and were with you, we, they. Add -ed to regular verbs to talk about finished actions. Describe what she did yesterday in ten sentences.
- **Words:** Yesterday, Last night, Ago, Attended, Visited, Arrived, Finished, Absent
- **Medical corner:** Admitted, Discharged, Examined, Operated, Prescribed, Recovered
- **Sound corner:** three sounds of -ed.
- **Dialogue:** Two classmates meet in the morning after a day's absence.
- **Speaking:** 5 sentences to record
- **In the app:** explainer 1:29, 93 exercises in the pool, homework of 100 mixed items.

### Class 8: Irregular Past Verbs & Weekend Stories

- **Focus:** Irregular past forms (go → went, buy → bought); did / did not
- **Goals:** Recall the past form of twenty high-frequency irregular verbs. Tell a short story in the past without switching back to the present. Use did and did not with irregular verbs correctly.
- **Words:** Went, Bought, Took, Met, Slept, Forgot, Brought, First / Then / After that, Finally
- **Medical corner:** Felt, Broke, Hurt, Caught, Lost weight, Took medicine
- **Sound corner:** linking words.
- **Dialogue:** Monday morning. Two students share what they did at the weekend.
- **Speaking:** 4 sentences to record
- **In the app:** explainer 1:28, 103 exercises in the pool, homework of 100 mixed items.

### Class 9: Future Plans & College Schedule

- **Focus:** will vs be going to; Present Continuous for fixed arrangements
- **Goals:** Use will for decisions made at the moment of speaking and for predictions. Use be going to for plans decided earlier. Talk about her goals for the coming semester for one minute.
- **Words:** Tomorrow, Next week, Plan to, Intend to, Semester, Specialise in, Probably, Goal
- **Medical corner:** Surgery, Follow-up, Test result, Vaccination, Check-up, Referral
- **Sound corner:** I'll and won't.
- **Dialogue:** A student talks to her academic mentor about the coming semester.
- **Speaking:** 5 sentences to record
- **In the app:** explainer 1:32, 98 exercises in the pool, homework of 100 mixed items.

- **Week check (10 minutes):** after this class, do a speaking round of 5 questions covering Classes 7 to 9; note slow answers.

## Week 4

### Class 10: Asking for Help & Polite Requests

- **Focus:** Can / Could / May / Would for requests and permission
- **Goals:** Turn a direct command into a polite request. Choose the right level of politeness for a teacher, a friend and a stranger. Ask a teacher to repeat or explain something without feeling embarrassed.
- **Words:** Assistance, Favour, Repeat, Explain, Borrow, Lend, Disturb, Clarify
- **Medical corner:** Pain, Fever, Cough, Rash, Allergy, Medicine
- **Sound corner:** polite intonation.
- **Dialogue:** After a lecture. The student approaches the teacher with a question.
- **Speaking:** 3 sentences to record
- **In the app:** explainer 1:30, 87 exercises in the pool, homework of 100 mixed items.

### Class 11: Describing People, Places & Things

- **Focus:** Adjectives and their position; There is / There are
- **Goals:** Place adjectives before the noun, and keep them singular. Use There is for one thing and There are for more than one. Describe her room, her college and a classmate in detail.
- **Words:** Spacious, Crowded, Quiet, Noisy, Helpful, Difficult, Clean / Dirty, Comfortable, Nearby
- **Medical corner:** Sharp, Dull, Mild, Severe, Swollen, Painful
- **Sound corner:** stressing adjectives.
- **Dialogue:** A video call home. The student describes her new life in Egypt to her mother.
- **Speaking:** 5 sentences to record
- **In the app:** explainer 1:32, 101 exercises in the pool, homework of 100 mixed items.

### Class 12: Month 1 Revision & Evaluation Test

- **Focus:** All of Month 1: present, past and future tenses; questions; requests; descriptions
- **Goals:** Switch between present, past and future without being prompted. Demonstrate every Month 1 structure in a live 10-minute viva. Identify, with the teacher, the two or three errors to focus on in Month 2.
- **Words:** revision of Month 1
- **Medical corner:** Nurse, Surgeon, Specialist, Ambulance, Prescription, Symptom
- **Sound corner:** review of v, w and th.
- **Dialogue:** The viva: five speaking tasks, five minutes each. Read each task aloud to the student.
- **Speaking:** 6 sentences to record
- **In the app:** explainer 1:20, 76 exercises in the pool, homework of 100 mixed items.

- **Evaluation:** written test (lesson plus homework items), a listening test (use listening exercises with new speakers), and a 2 minute speaking test: introduce yourself, describe your day, ask two questions, make one polite request.
